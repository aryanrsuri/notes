I really do want to be able to concentrate for longer periods of time, and at once have some notes [[attention-and-focus]] on this.

The enlightement anglo-philosophers feel like a mentality is shaped solely by `experience` in the sense of a mind is a clay, and experierience _rains_ down upon us to mold into our disposition. But why then do the dogs of the Vacitan have no more disposition to act with the accordance of statues, but rather the odors of the statues become more relevant to them? `Interest` is the core lever here. Interest is the filter (Since 1mil things can be in my periphery, but my interest filters them)

Furthermore so many people have tried to understand the limits of concentration. Is it 6 "objects", or 4, or some transcendental number, something that you can either train or is ingrainied? This is of less value. Obviously, for example, composition similar objects in _units_ (like counting beans on a floor, or conting clumps of beans) will obviously increase object focus. 

Always, our minds must make effort to concentrate on many things at once, even if habitual, and the time-effecient gains are erroded by performance-effecients [1]
