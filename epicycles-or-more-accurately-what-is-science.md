## Epicycles

In plotemic astronomy, since there wasn't a formal paradigm of rotaion around the Sun, there were what I would call "abstractions"
built to explain the apparent variations in motion of other heavenly bodies, such as retrograde: epicycles. The idea is simply adding
more small ellipticals to the revolution of a body. 

Of course G ~ 1/s^2 is more formal, simple, and true in the true model of the universe, but interestengly, If you take to the limit of
\inf epicycles you can "technically" approximate the true motion of the body, as that is essentially a fourier analysis, but of course it 
still describes the natural object of astronomy wrong (centre of the universe), and doesn't provide a basis for more fundemental maths (i.e. Netwons 
laws of motion --> Relativity)

What does this tell us (This kind of topic is certainly [[what-interests-me]])

1. There always exists a fundemental "paradigm" about some category.
1.1 A paradigm is a structure that tries to explain NATURAL objects in this category.
1.2 All logic is relative to this paradigm.
1.2.1 Thus, within a paradigm knowledge can be gained continously (normal science) that is correct, but absurd from the perspective of a different paradigm.
2. All knowledge gaining work within a paradigm either confirms (evidence) or denies (anomoly) this paradigm.
2.2 It will be more obvious that the current paradigm does not model the NATURAL objects within a category, if an increasing complex of "abstractions" is required to explain anomolies.
2.2.1 An abstraction is a function that attempts to explain a simple process with a more complex one, often to satisfy more constraints.
2.1 If these anomolies are consistent and do not abate with scrutiny, they can lead to a new paradigm.
2.3 Thus, shifting to a new paradigm might be valuable.
3. While paradigms do not inherently start more accurate at modeling the NATURAL objects within the category, they: 
    (a) Require less abstractions to explain anomolies (i.e. satisfy occam's razor)
    (b) Always end with a more accurate model of the NATURAL objects within the category 
4. Thus we can say that a science exists if 
    (a) It's focus is to explain NATURAL objects within some category
    (b) Shows a dynamic nature in which old paradigms are replaced with newer ones
    (c) Essentially leaps of knowledge exists through the _discontinuities_ of paradigms
4.1 One corollary of this, is a non-science is one in which all knowledge work either
    (a) Cannot demarcate between an evidence or anomoly of a paradigm 
    (b) Cannot hold past paradigms accountable (either there is 1 paradigm, or all are equivilant)
